Key Roles of a CPA in Taxes:

Tax Preparation
CPAs prepare federal, state, and local tax returns for individuals, businesses, and organizations, ensuring compliance with all tax laws and regulations.

Tax Planning
They help clients strategically plan their finances to minimize tax liability, take advantage of deductions and credits, and avoid penalties.

IRS Representation
CPAs can represent clients before the IRS in audits, appeals, or disputes, unlike non-licensed tax preparers (only CPAs, attorneys, and Enrolled Agents have this right).

Financial Consulting
CPAs often provide advice on broader financial matters that intersect with taxes—like retirement planning, investments, or starting a business.

Audit and Assurance Services
While not strictly a tax service, CPAs also conduct audits of financial statements, which can be relevant when businesses face tax-related scrutiny.

Why Hire a CPA for Taxes?

They are licensed and regulated by state boards of accountancy.

They must follow strict ethical guidelines and complete continuing education.

They are trained in complex tax issues like business taxes, multi-state filings, and estate taxes.
